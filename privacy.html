Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026 · Applies to Guessless: Minesweeper for iPhone and iPad

Guessless has no account and no server of ours. Your games, stats and settings stay on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud. The game is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google's ad software collects some data to show and measure those ads, as described below. Buying Remove Ads turns all ads off.

Your games and progress

On your device

Your saved games, statistics, daily medals and streaks, level, achievements, lesson progress and settings are stored inside the app on your iPhone or iPad. We never receive any of it.

In your own iCloud

If iCloud is on for your Apple Account, your record (level, statistics, daily medals and streaks, achievements and lesson progress) is also kept in your own iCloud, so a new or second iPhone or iPad picks it up. It is stored by Apple under your account; we cannot see it and it never reaches a server of ours. Unfinished boards and settings stay on the device. To stop it, turn off iCloud for Guessless under Settings › your name › iCloud. If you delete Guessless, the copy on the device is deleted with it; the copy in your iCloud stays under your Apple Account, so reinstalling brings your record back.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

What Google collects

Unless you have bought Remove Ads, Guessless shows ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To show ads, limit how often they repeat, measure them and prevent fraud, the SDK may collect:

  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location;
  • device identifiers: the identifier for vendors and, only if you allow tracking, the advertising identifier (IDFA);
  • advertising data, such as which ads were shown and whether you tapped them;
  • product interactions, such as app launches and ad video views;
  • diagnostics, such as crash logs and performance data (launch time, hangs).

This data goes to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. See also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Tracking is your choice

After your first finished game, iOS asks whether Guessless may track you across apps and websites of other companies. If you allow it, Google may use the advertising identifier to show ads based on your interests. If you don't, ads still appear but are not personalized with that identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Europe, the UK and Switzerland

In these regions, Google's consent form asks first whether ads may be personalized and how your data may be used. No ad is requested before you answer. You can review or change your choice at any time in the app under Settings › Ad privacy choices.

Rewarded ads

Some ads give you something in the game (an extra hint, undoing a mine, or another life in Endless Descent). They play only when you tap for them.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, Guessless sends your best times (wins without hints or undo), your Endless Descent depth and your number of wins to Apple's leaderboards, where other players can see them with your Game Center nickname. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we receive only the scores. Guessless never asks you to sign in when it starts, only when you open Leaderboards.

Purchases

Remove Ads is an optional one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Guessless never sees your payment details or Apple ID; Apple gives developers only anonymous sales totals. The app may also show Apple's standard rating prompt; iOS decides whether it appears.

When you share

Sharing a daily result opens the iOS share sheet with a short text (the date, your medal, your time and streak). It goes only where you choose to send it.

Permissions

Guessless asks for two permissions, each only when it is needed: tracking, described above, and notifications, only if you turn on the daily reminder. The reminders are scheduled on your device itself; nothing about them is sent anywhere. It does not use your camera, photos, microphone, location services, contacts or health data.

Children

Guessless is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. We collect no personal information ourselves at all.
