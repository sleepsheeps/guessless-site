Guessless

Minesweeper, no guessing.

Every board in Guessless is checked by a solver before you see it: from your first tap, it can be cleared using only what the numbers prove. If you are stuck, there is always a next step, and a hint shows you where and why.

Common questions

I hit a mine. Wasn't that a guess?

No board in Guessless needs a guess, so a mine means that cell was not proven safe yet. Tap Hint next time you are unsure: it outlines a cell that can be proven and highlights the numbers that prove it. After a mine you can retry the same board, or undo the move by watching a short ad (the mine then gets a flag).

How do I place a flag?

Long press a cell, or switch the button under the board from Dig to Flag. Settings lets you make the long press shorter or longer. With Quick open on, tapping a number whose mines are all flagged opens the cells around it.

I'm new to minesweeper.

Open Learn Minesweeper on the home screen: seven short lessons, one move each, from "numbers count mines" to the 1-2-1 pattern. Together they cover what every board needs.

How do hints work?

You get three free hints a day. After that, each hint plays a short ad first, and only when you ask for it. With Remove Ads, extra hints and undos need no ad.

How does the daily board work?

Everyone gets the same board each day. Solve it without hints or undo for a gold medal (under 3:00) or silver (under 6:00); any other solve earns bronze. Solving on the day builds your streak. Past days in the calendar can still be played for medals, but they don't extend the streak.

Can it remind me to play?

Yes. Turn on the daily reminder in Settings and pick a time. It is skipped on days you have already solved the daily board, and tapping it opens today's board.

What is Endless Descent?

Dig down through layer after layer of no-guess boards. You have three hearts: a mine costs one. When they run out, you can watch an ad once per run to keep going.

When do I see ads?

A banner under the board, and now and then a full-screen ad when you leave a won game: never in your first games, never after a loss, at most every other game and a few minutes apart. Ads that give you something (a hint, an undo, a revive) play only when you tap for them.

What does Remove Ads do?

It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It removes every ad, makes extra hints, undos and revives free, and unlocks all board themes (otherwise unlocked by leveling up). On a new device, open Remove Ads and tap Restore purchases. Purchases are handled by Apple; refunds are at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Leaderboards don't open.

Leaderboards use Apple's Game Center. Sign in under Settings › Game Center on your device, then tap Leaderboards again. Best times count on Easy, Medium, Hard and Extreme (first tries without hints or undo), plus today's daily board and your deepest Endless Descent.

Will I lose my progress?

No. Your level, stats, medals, streaks and achievements are stored on your device and, when iCloud is on, in your own iCloud: a new iPhone or iPad with the same Apple Account picks them up, and two devices merge their records. There is no account of ours to sign in to. Unfinished boards and settings stay on each device. Without iCloud, the record lives only on the device and is deleted if you delete the app.
