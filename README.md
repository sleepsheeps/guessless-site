# Guessless site

Support page and privacy policy for the Guessless: Minesweeper iOS app, served with GitHub Pages at
https://sleepsheeps.github.io/guessless-site/

App Store Connect has them as the support URL and the privacy policy URL. `privacy.html` must
resolve, or App Store review stops on it.

The privacy policy describes what the app actually does: local storage only, Google AdMob ads (with
the data Google's SDK collects, tracking only with ATT permission, Google's consent form in the
EEA/UK/Switzerland), Game Center leaderboards, and the Remove Ads purchase through Apple. If the app
changes any of that (another ad network, analytics, a permission), update `privacy.html` and the App
Store privacy label in the same change.
